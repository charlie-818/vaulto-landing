The private markets that once settled quietly between funds and their limited
partners are now visible in near real time. Vaulto's order book prices implied
valuations for the largest late-stage private companies continuously, giving us
a dataset that did not exist a market cycle ago. This report is the first cut of
what that data shows.

## Key findings

- Implied valuations for the top pre-IPO names moved more in the last quarter
  than in the prior year of primary rounds.
- Secondary liquidity is concentrating in a handful of names while the long tail
  stays illiquid.
- The gap between last primary round and current implied price is widest for the
  fastest-growing AI infrastructure companies.

## Methodology

Implied valuations are derived from Vaulto's own secondary order book. For each
company we take the volume-weighted midpoint price over the reporting window,
multiply by the most recently disclosed fully diluted share count, and reconcile
against the last known primary round where available.

- **Window:** trailing 90 days unless otherwise noted.
- **Price basis:** volume-weighted midpoint of resting bids and asks.
- **Share counts:** most recent public or company-confirmed fully diluted figure.
- **Exclusions:** names with fewer than a minimum number of trades in the window
  are omitted to avoid stale marks.

Figures are estimates for informational purposes only and are not investment
advice, an offer, or a solicitation. Verify against primary sources before
relying on any number in this report.

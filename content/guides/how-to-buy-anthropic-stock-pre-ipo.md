# How to Buy Anthropic Stock Before the IPO

Anthropic, the AI safety company behind the Claude models, has raised at rapidly rising private valuations and remains firmly private. If you want exposure before any IPO, you have to work within the same tight constraints that apply to every hot private company.

This guide covers the realistic routes and where tokenized pre-IPO access fits.

## Why Anthropic is hard to buy

Like its peers, Anthropic sells equity through negotiated funding rounds to strategic and institutional investors. There is no public market, no ticker, and the shareholder base is deliberately narrow. Retail investors have historically had no direct way in.

## The routes available today

- **Primary rounds.** Reserved for large investors and strategic partners. Not accessible to the public.
- **Pre-IPO and AI-thematic funds.** Some funds hold Anthropic in a diversified basket. You buy the fund, typically as an accredited investor with a high minimum.
- **Secondary transfers.** Occasionally existing holders sell through brokered deals, gated to accredited investors and subject to company approval.

## Where tokenized pre-IPO access fits

Tokenization represents pre-IPO economic exposure as an on-chain token tracking a private-company outcome, rather than registered shares. The aim is to widen access with lower minimums, transparent on-chain pricing, and faster settlement than a private secondary.

A token is not the same as being on Anthropic's cap table. Read the [compliance reference](/compliance) to understand how such instruments are treated.

## What to check before you invest

1. **What you actually own** — shares, fund units, or an outcome token.
2. **Liquidity** — whether you can exit before a listing at all.
3. **Eligibility** — accreditation and jurisdiction rules for each route.
4. **Fees and lockups** — carry, spreads, and transfer restrictions.

Pre-IPO investing is high risk and illiquid. This is not investment advice — consult a licensed adviser.

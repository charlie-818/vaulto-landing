## The short version

Forge Global is built for accredited investors who want to own actual shares of
late-stage private companies and are comfortable with high minimums and long
holding periods. Vaulto is built for anyone who wants **liquid, on-chain
exposure** to how those companies are valued — without the accreditation gate,
the paperwork, or the lockup.

You are not buying equity on Vaulto. You are taking a position on a company's
implied IPO valuation, derived from Polymarket prediction markets and settled in
USDC. That trade-off — synthetic exposure instead of real shares — is what makes
Vaulto liquid and open to everyone.

## When Forge Global makes more sense

If you specifically need to own the underlying shares — for a cap-table stake,
long-term equity ownership, or an allocation an accredited fund requires — a
secondary marketplace like Forge is the right tool. Vaulto does not give you
equity, voting rights, or a position on the company's cap table.

## When Vaulto makes more sense

If you want to express a view on a private company's valuation, move in and out
freely, start small, and avoid accreditation paperwork, Vaulto is designed for
exactly that. Positions are liquid, non-custodial, and priced off live
prediction-market odds.

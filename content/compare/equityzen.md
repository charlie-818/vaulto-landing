## The short version

EquityZen is built for accredited investors who want pre-IPO **equity** and are
willing to lock capital into a fund until the company exits. Vaulto is built for
anyone who wants **liquid exposure** to a private company's valuation, without
accreditation, fund paperwork, or a lockup.

The core difference is the same one that runs through every Vaulto comparison:
EquityZen gets you (indirect) ownership of real shares; Vaulto gets you a liquid,
tradeable position on a company's implied IPO valuation, derived from Polymarket
and settled in USDC.

## When EquityZen makes more sense

If your goal is to actually own pre-IPO equity — for long-term ownership or an
allocation your portfolio strategy requires — and you are accredited and
comfortable locking up capital, EquityZen's fund model fits that need. Vaulto
does not give you shares, dividends, or fund distributions.

## When Vaulto makes more sense

If you want to start small, avoid accreditation and fund lockups, and keep the
freedom to exit at any time, Vaulto is designed for that. Positions are liquid,
non-custodial, and priced off live prediction-market odds.

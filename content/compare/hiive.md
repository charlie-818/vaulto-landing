## The short version

Hiive is built for accredited investors who want to trade actual private-company
shares through a bid/ask order book and are willing to wait for a matched
counterparty and a manual transfer. Vaulto is built for anyone who wants
**instant, liquid exposure** to a company's valuation, priced off live
prediction-market odds and settled in USDC.

You are not buying shares on Vaulto. You are taking a position on a company's
implied IPO valuation, which is what lets Vaulto stay liquid and open to
everyone — no counterparty matching, no accreditation, no transfer paperwork.

## When Hiive makes more sense

If you need to own the underlying shares and want to work an order book to hit a
specific price — and you are accredited and comfortable with settlement delays —
Hiive's marketplace is the right tool. Vaulto does not give you equity or a place
on the company's cap table.

## When Vaulto makes more sense

If you want exposure that fills instantly, has no minimum, requires no
accreditation, and can be exited at any time, Vaulto is designed for that.
Positions are liquid, non-custodial, and priced off live prediction-market odds.
